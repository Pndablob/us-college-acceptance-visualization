# A visualization of US university acceptance rates
This project visualized the acceptance rates of US universities, with a deeper dive into the top 20 universities based on the [US World News Report Best National University Rankings](https://www.usnews.com/best-colleges/rankings/national-universities).

The data was sourced from the [US Department of Education College Scorecard](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/) and includes information on institutional characteristics, admissions, enrollment, student aid, costs, and student outcomes. This focus was on visualizing acceptance rates and comparing it with standardized test scores.

The data was cleaned, processed, and organized using **Pandas** and visualized using **Matplotlib** and **Google Sheets**
